Delete Your Data — LEFTY

Applies to the Android app LEFTY (com.hsn.lefty), developed by HSN.

What data the app keeps

LEFTY stores all of your game data only on your device: level, coins, best score, and whether you have seen the tutorial. This data is never sent to any server and the developer has no access to it.

The only data collected outside the app is by Google AdMob (the ad SDK), which uses your device's advertising ID to serve ads.

How to delete your data

  1. Game data (level, coins, scores): uninstall the LEFTY app from your device. All locally stored game data is permanently and automatically deleted on uninstall — there is no copy on any server and no retention period.
  2. Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob): on your Android device open Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (location of this setting varies by device). You can also review and reset ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Data kept after deletion

None. After uninstalling the app and deleting your advertising ID, no data related to you remains with the developer — the developer holds no personal data at any point.

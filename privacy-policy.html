Privacy Policy — LEFTY

Effective date: November 6, 2026

LEFTY ("the game", "the app") is a single-player leftward-gravity merge puzzle game developed by HSN.

Data we collect

The developer of this game collects no personal data — no accounts, no analytics, no trackers of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners collect and process certain data to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • Approximate location (coarse, non-precise)
  • App interactions (ads shown, clicked)

This data is used for advertising and may be shared with ad partners. You can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete/reset advertising ID) or via Google's Ads Settings. For details on how Google uses data, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

No internet access is required to play — the game runs fully offline. Ads load only when a connection is available.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device using app-local storage under the ly_ namespace:

  • ly_progress — level, coins, best score
  • ly_tut_done — whether you have seen the tutorial

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data. You can also reset progress by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, but it collects no personal data from anyone. Ad content follows Google's ad policies.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
